Work Rate Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page combines the times you type, assuming every worker or pipe keeps one steady rate. It does not know about slowdowns, handoffs, setup time or whether the numbers you typed are right. Check those where they apply.

The together truth

How long 2 to 6 workers take on one job, from each worker's time alone in hours (up to 2 decimals). Separate times with commas or spaces.

The other truth

One worker's time alone and the time together give the other worker's time alone.

The pipe truth

An inlet that fills a tank and an outlet that drains it, both open.
