Work Rate Math

A 6-hour worker and a 3-hour worker finish one job in 2 hours, not 4.5.

Boundary: arithmetic only Work Rate Math combines the times you type, assuming every worker or pipe keeps one steady rate. It does not know about slowdowns, handoffs, setup time or whether the numbers you typed are right. Check those where they apply.

The together truth

Working together, 6 h and 3 h finish one job in exactly 2 h, or 2 h 0 min to the nearest minute.

The other truth

If one worker alone takes 6 h and both together take 2 h, the other worker alone takes exactly 3 h, or 3 h 0 min to the nearest minute.

The pipe truth

An inlet that fills the tank in 3 h and an outlet that drains it in 6 h, both open, fill an empty tank in exactly 6 h, or 6 h 0 min to the nearest minute.
